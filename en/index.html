    Who it's for

    For owners and operations

    The offer is aimed at business owners and people responsible for operations who want to systematize processes that consume team time, scale activities, or optimize team cost — many tasks in a company can be handled without handing bots strategic decisions or high-risk actions.

    Typical starting team: 2–3 bots for one workflow; launch in about 3 days.

    Control

    How we ensure control

    A deployment model based on rules, human approvals (Human in the Loop), and escalation when there is uncertainty or risk.

    01

    Clear rules

    Every bot has a defined role, permission scope, and operating rules — no opaque “black box.”

    02

    Human control

    Outbound messages, payments, and other irreversible actions require your attention and approval.

    03

    Escalation

    When a bot lacks sufficient confidence, it hands the matter to a human. In the feedback loop it learns from your decisions so it can take even better actions on its own.
