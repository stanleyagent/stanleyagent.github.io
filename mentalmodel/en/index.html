    What a mental model is

    At getStanley it’s a map: processes → decisions → bots + HITL

    We don’t write an encyclopedia. We extract what’s already in your head: steps, who decides, when the bot asks, and what is irreversible.

    Steps

    Processes → steps

    What happens in order — no excess docs, only what the bot should execute.

    Decisions

    Who decides / when the bot asks

    Points where a human approves, or the bot escalates instead of guessing.

    Boundaries

    What is irreversible

    Shipments, payments, promises to the customer — always on the human side.

    Fit

    A fit when

    • you own a company of ~5–50 people,
    • you keep processes in your head (or in email and your head),
    • you want bots to take the routine and leave decisions with you.

    Not a fit when

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I need ready-made process documentation?

    No. We build the model from conversation and what’s in your head. Documentation is not a start requirement.

    How much of my time as the owner will this take?

    A short brief and mapping sessions for one process — not a multi-month project. Typical start for one workflow: about 3 days after scope is set.

    Will the bot do something irreversible without me knowing?

    No. Payments, shipments, promises to the customer, and exceptions require human approval. Without HITL we don’t run the model.

    What if the model is incomplete?

    The bot escalates when unsure. We close gaps in the model at later check-ins — iteration is part of the process, not a failure.
